Wiring Rules Reader

Turns your PDF of AS/NZS 3000 into a searchable, bookmarkable book.

Open your copy of the Standard

Choose the PDF (or drag it here). It's read and indexed on this device and never uploaded anywhere. The first open takes about half a minute while the contents, tables and index are built.

On this device

Clickable contentsEvery clause in the contents, index and "see Clause…" reference jumps straight to the page.
Tables drawerAll tables in one list: filter by number or name, star the ones you use daily.
Search & jumpFull-text search, or type 3.4.2, Table 8.1 or p 158.
BookmarksSave pages with your own names. They stay on this device and work offline.
